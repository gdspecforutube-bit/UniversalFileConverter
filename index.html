Frequently asked questions

Is my data safe?

Yes. Conversion runs locally in your browser, and your files are never uploaded or stored on our servers.

Which file formats are supported?

Supported formats include JPG, PNG, WEBP, HEIC, AVIF, SVG, PDF, DOCX, TXT, MP3, WAV, AAC, FLAC, OGG, M4A, MP4 and WEBM.

Is there a limit on file size?
